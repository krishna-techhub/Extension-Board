Extension Board Viva Rules: Key Facts
1. Series Row: Ek bhi plug bahar nikaalne ya switch band karne par poora extension board band ho jata hai!
2. Parallel Row: Koi bhi plug nikaalo, baaki sockets me light/fan bilkul normal chalta rehta hai!
3. X-Ray View: Top plate ko transparent karke internal Phase (Red), Neutral (Black) aur Earth (Green) wiring live dekhein.

1
Modular Box Layout (Dual Row Design):

Top row par 1 Master Switch aur 3 Sockets lagayein (Series Extension Demo). Bottom row par 1 Master Switch aur 3 Sockets lagayein (Normal Parallel Extension Board).

2
Series Row Wiring (Daisy Chain):

Power Phase $\to$ Series Switch $\to$ Socket 1 Live. Socket 1 ka Neutral wire Socket 2 ke Live me jodein. Socket 2 ka Neutral Socket 3 ke Live me jodein. Socket 3 ka Neutral wapas Negative return me jodein. (Single unbroken loop!)

3
Parallel Row Wiring (Domestic Standard):

Phase wire switch se nikaalkar ek Common Live Bus me jodein jo teeno sockets ke Live (L) terminals me branch hoti hai. Ek Common Neutral Bus teeno sockets ke Neutral (N) terminals me jodein. Green wire se teeno Earth pins ground karein.

4
Live Demonstration to Judges/Teachers:

Pehle Parallel row me 3 plugs lagayein — teeno full bright jalenge. Ek plug nikaalne par bhi baaki 2 sockets me koi farq nahi padega. Phir Series row me ek plug nikaalein — pura board turant band ho jayega! Teachers will be impressed.

Q1: Real Extension Board aur ghar ki wiring hamesha Parallel me kyu hoti hai?
Ans: Do main reasons hain: (1) Har socket ko full 240V/9V voltage milta hai taaki har appliance full speed aur power pe chale, (2) Sabhi sockets independent hote hain — agar ek appliance unplug ho ya switch off ho, to baaki sockets me power chalti rehti hai.
Q2: Agar extension cord ko Series me wire kar diya jaye to kya hoga?
Ans: Do badi problems aayengi: (1) Voltage sabhi sockets me divide ho jayega ($240V / 3 = 80V$ each), jisse bulbs dim jalenge aur fridge/laptop on bhi nahi honge. (2) Agar ek socket se plug bahar nikala jaye ya switch off kiya jaye, to poora extension cord band ho jayega.
Q3: Indian 3-Pin Socket me upar wala Earth pin baki do se bada aur lamba kyu hota hai?
Ans: Earth pin lamba isliye hota hai taaki plug insert karte waqt appliance sabse pehle ground/earth se connect ho, aur unplug karte waqt sabse aakhri me disconnect ho (Safety first principle). Bada isliye hota hai taaki galti se Live ya Neutral me na ghus sake aur metallic chassis leakage current safely earth ho sake.
Q4: Parallel circuit me total resistance add hone ke bajay kam kyu ho jati hai?
Ans: Formula $1/R_{eq} = 1/R_1 + 1/R_2 + 1/R_3$ hota hai. Kyunki current ke flow hone ke liye multiple parallel channels (branches) mil jate hain, isliye total current $I_{total}$ badh jati hai aur net effective resistance ($R_{eq} = V / I_{total}$) circuit ke sabse chhote resistor se bhi kam ho jati hai.
